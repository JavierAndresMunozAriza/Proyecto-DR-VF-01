\subsection{Evidence}

Every figure of this report comes from a file in \texttt{evidence/}, described in its \texttt{README.md}: the
console logs (\texttt{logs/}), the output of the PC tests and of the cross-compilation, the cold-start time
measured by hand (\texttt{field\_ttff\_manual.csv}), the conditions of the tests
(\texttt{field\_conditions.md}) and the offsets of the static test from its mean
(\texttt{static\_offsets.csv}). The two field walks are transcribed, without coordinates, in
\texttt{field\_walk\_open\_sky.csv} and \texttt{field\_walk\_signal\_loss.csv}. The raw captures of the field tests contain the location of the board and are
not published.

\subsection{References}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Nordic Semiconductor, \emph{nRF9151 Product Specification}, GNSS section, Table 2:
        \url{https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf9151/page/gps.html}. Sensitivity, time to first fix and
        horizontal accuracy (CEP50) of the receiver.
  \item Nordic Semiconductor, \emph{nRF Modem library}, GNSS interface document and modem header file, from
        \dNcs{}. Data kept in non-volatile memory and its deletion to simulate a cold start; format of the
        position and satellite data.
  \item Nordic Semiconductor, \dNcs{} documentation for the Thingy:91~X. Components of the board and the
        connectivity bridge of the nRF5340.
  \item \emph{Technical Report of the DR-VF-01 collar} (in Spanish), \texttt{docs/DR-VF-01\_Memoria\_Tecnica.pdf}.
        PRD and SRD requirements to which the traceability refers.
\end{enumerate}
