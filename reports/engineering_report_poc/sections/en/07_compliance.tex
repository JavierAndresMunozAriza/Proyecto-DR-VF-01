\subsection{From requirements to characteristics}

A \emph{requirement} says what the product must do. The \emph{compliance matrix} above says how it was
verified and whether it was met. A \emph{characteristic}, as in a datasheet, says what the product does, with
the measured value and the conditions. The next table gathers only what was measured in the PoC. A value
appears here even if it does not reach the related requirement: the right-hand column points to the
requirement, and the matrix above gives the verdict. Nothing that was not measured is presented as a
characteristic.
