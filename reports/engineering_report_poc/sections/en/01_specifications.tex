of the Technical Report; no new requirements were invented. When a criterion is a design decision of the
PoC and does not come from a requirement, it is marked as \emph{new}. The results are presented in the
results section and compared with this table in the compliance matrix.

\subsection{Out of scope of the PoC}

The PoC does not cover, and this report claims nothing about: the acoustic stage and the deterrent pulse
(F-03, SR-30), galvanic isolation with an optocoupler (F-04, NF-02), LoRa telemetry (F-05), the web
configuration server (F-06), power management, charging and protections (F-07 to F-11, NF-01), wake-up by
motion with the IMU (F-12) and storing the perimeter in non-volatile memory (NF-07). These requirements will
be verified on the PCB.
