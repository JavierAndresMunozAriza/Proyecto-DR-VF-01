## 3. Verified OK

- **The 50 mV/m lead is correct arithmetically:** 50 mV/m at 3 m gives EIRP = (0.05 × 3)²/30 = 0.75 mW = −1.25 dBm, against +22 + 2 = +24 dBm planned (25 dB over). It applies only if the device falls outside the hopping, digital and hybrid category (RF-01).
- **Power level vs the 915-928 MHz free-use limits (draft annex):** 158 mW conducted is under 250 mW (25-49 channels) and under 1 W (50 or more channels, or digital). The antenna is 2 dBi, under the 6 dBi threshold. PSD at +22 dBm is 22 − 10·log(125/3) = 5.8 dBm/3 kHz at BW125 and −0.2 dBm/3 kHz at BW500, both under the 8 dBm/3 kHz limit.
- **50 Ω microstrip math as written:** 53 mil on 0.73 mm with εr 4.4 and t = 35 µm gives 50.3 Ω (Hammerstad-Jensen; the repo says 50.1 Ω). Over 0.63-0.83 mm it gives 46.0-54.2 Ω (repo: 45.8-54.0), and over εr 4.2-4.6 it gives 51.3-49.3 Ω. The figure is consistent; only the routed geometry differs (RF-04).
- **Bottom reference plane:** solid under both RF traces (33 of 33 samples per trace over poured copper), with voids only under the J4 and J5 bodies.
- **Nothing under U6 or U7:** no track or via inside either module body on either layer (live PcbDoc).
- **RF nets have no stubs:** LORA_ANT touches only U7.21 and J5.2, and GNSS_RF only U6.11 and J4.2. Both are top-layer only, with no layer change (Espressif HDG p26: no vias on RF).
- **Distances:** U6 to U7 is about 32 mm. U6 is more than 45 mm from the CN3791 stage (U3 at y 2160, L1 at y 1621) and from the TP4056 (U2), satisfying the heat-source rule (IM p80).
- **MAX-M10S RF front end:** RF_IN is 50 Ω with a built-in DC block (DS p12), so a passive patch needs no bias. LNA_EN and VCC_RF left NC is correct for a passive antenna (IM p79-80). The default low-gain LNA mode suits a design with no external gain; bypass mode is only for 10-15 dB or more of external gain (IM p77-78). V_BCKP open is allowed ("If the hardware backup mode is not used, leave the V_BCKP pin open", IM p74). VCC has only the 0 Ω R35 in series, within the 0.2 Ω limit (IM p73).
- **LoRa supply:** 3V3_LORA via R36 (0 Ω), with 10 µF + 100 nF + 22 µF and a 100 µF 1206 not fitted (`sheets.py:197-206`). VCC abs max is 3.7 V against a 3.3 V rail (manual p12). TX is 118 mA at +22 dBm (SX1262 DS p17), under the OCP of 140 mA after SetPaConfig (DS Table 5-2 p32) and the LDO's 1 A. +22 dBm needs VBAT ≥ 3.1 V (DS Table 4-4 p31); the guard case is FW F-23.
- **Module sensitivity claim:** −125 dBm at SF7/BW125 (manual p8) against the chip's −124 dBm boosted (DS p19), within 1 dB.
- **u.FL:** 50 Ω, VSWR ≤ 1.3 to 3 GHz with 1.13 mm cable (Hirose catalog p3). The WROOM-1U connector is compatible with U.FL and MHF I (WROOM DS p43).
- **8 km geometry:** the radio horizon (k = 4/3, 1.2 m and 3 m) is 11.6 km, so the limit at 8 km is Fresnel clearance and ground reflection, not curvature.
- **LoRa module layout guidance:** nothing under the module, and top-copper ground if anything must cross (manual p13-14). The live layout complies.

## 4. Unverified (not findings)

- **Final text of Anexo 1** of ANE Res. 105/2020 as amended. The 915-928 MHz conditions above (50 mV/m RCA row, at least 50 channels and 0.4 s per 20 s for hopping, at least 500 kHz 6 dB bandwidth for digital modulation, hybrid rules) come from the ANE **draft** annex. The final annex PDF (avancejuridico link) returned 404. The structure matches the 2022 MinTIC/ANE slides, but the numbers are not confirmed in force. Res. 028/2026 does not mention free use for 902-915 MHz.
- Whether the ANE treats LoRaWAN 125 kHz operation as "híbrido", as FCC practice does, and whether LoRa BW500 meets the 500 kHz 6 dB bandwidth definition (needs a measurement).
- Secondary sources disagree on the region: the TTN wiki lists Colombia as US902-928 and a TTN forum thread disputes it. RP002-1.0.3 (primary) says AU915-928. RP002-1.0.4 and 1.0.5 were not checked.
- Antenna isolation between LoRa, Wi-Fi and the GNSS patch in this enclosure. The patch's out-of-band response at 915 and 2440 MHz is not published for HX-TWGPS-184-CV0.
- LoRa antenna (A2) type, gain and pattern; Wi-Fi FPC gain; GNSS patch bandwidth and the ground plane it was tuned for.
- Body loss on the animal (6-15 dB assumed), polarization loss, Huila terrain, and the receiver antenna height.
- SF10 sensitivity of −132 dBm is derived from the SF12 noise figure and the usual LoRa SNR limits; the SX1262 DS table lists only SF7 and SF12 at BW125.
- The real JLCPCB 2-layer 0.8 mm dielectric thickness and εr, and the CPWG impedance (the closed-form spread is 37-46 Ω; a field solver is needed).
- The exact position of the WROOM-1U U.FL on the module (Fig. 10-2 did not extract), and the actual level of ESP32 or SX1262 spurs at 1560 and 1600 MHz.
- Whether the u.FL compatible part (Molex 20279-001E clone, COMPRAS O4) meets the Hirose ratings.

## 5. Cross-domain notes

- **FIRMWARE:** (1) The radio profile must change (RF-01): an AU915 channel list or BW500, dwell ≤ 0.4 s, and never 915.0 MHz. This turns F-06's 1.3 s SF12 airtime from a power issue into a legality issue. At SF10/BW125, a 20 B payload is about 0.37 s (own estimate from the F-06 formula) and fits the 0.4 s dwell. (2) Change BDS B1I to B1C (RF-06). (3) Until isolation is measured: no LoRa TX and no Wi-Fi while the GNSS is on (RF-02), and no SoftAP during a LoRa TX (RF-14).
