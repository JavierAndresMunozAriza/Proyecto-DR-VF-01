### PI-1 detail: first power-up sequence and where to measure

0. Unpowered: resistance VBAT-GND, 3V3-GND, VBUS-GND and VCC_SOLAR-GND (no shorts). Measure the purchased battery and panel cables with a DMM and compare with the "+" silkscreen (PD-1).
1. Battery only, emulated: bench supply 3.7 V, current limit 100 mA, on J2; no panel, no USB. Expect 3V3 = 3.25-3.35 V (TLV757P +-1.5 %, DS p5) and a monotonic ramp of about 550 us (scope).
2. Before plugging USB, measure VBUS at the connector with the ESP32 awake and asleep (PC-2). Program over USB-C with J2 still powered. A source-only bench supply cannot sink the TP4056 current: set it near 4.0 V or use the real cell.
3. RNF-03: real cell (or emulator) on J2 through the shunt with bypass; J3 and USB open. Expected 115-137 uA (PH-1). Repeat warm (40-50 C) because D1/D2 and the typical-only items rise. Isolate contributions by state (GNSS standby vs running, IMU power-down vs 12.5 Hz); the 3V3 rail separately needs the 0 ohm links another auditor proposed.
4. Solar path, with the battery connected first: bench supply on J3 at 8 V, current limit 0.25 A. Ramp VCC from 5 V up: charge must start between 6.05 and 6.25 V (GPIO41 goes low). VCS across RCS 110-130 mV (0.234-0.277 A). Lower the current limit below demand: VCC must sit at 5.80-6.25 V (MPPT). Near full: CV 4.158-4.247 V and termination near 41 mA. Dim the input to check PA-U2. Measure D1, D2 and L temperatures.
5. USB charge with the cell near 3.0-3.2 V: charge current 429-606 mA (PB-2), VBUS at the TP4056 pin under load >= 4.35 V, TP4056 case temperature after 10 minutes (PB-1). Plug and unplug a few times with a scope on VCC (PB-3).
6. TX droop: emulator at 3.3 V with a 0.3 ohm series resistor (cell + PCM estimate); scope VBAT at J2 and 3V3 at the LoRa pad during a +22 dBm TX; the rail must stay >= 3.0 V (PE-2).
7. All three sources: total current into J2 <= 0.885 A; check that the TP4056 terminates once the ESP32 sleeps (PC-1).
8. Outdoors with the real panel on the collar: Voc and Vmp at noon (PA-2) before fixing R3/R4.

### A. CN3791
- VCC range: panel Voc about 8 V (brief) and up to 2 x Voc = 16 V hot-plug ringing estimate, against CN3791 4.5-28 V operating and 30 V abs max (DS p3, p4), AP2301 Vds -20 V (DS p1), C1 35 V, ceramics 25 V. OK.
- Inductor rule L > 5 x (VCC - VBAT) uH (DS p8): worst VCC 8 V with VBAT 2.5 V (deep discharge) gives 27.5 uH; even VBAT = 0 (no battery) gives 40 uH; 47 uH K (+-10 %, CY54 DS p2) min 42.3 uH > 40 uH. OK.
- Ripple dI = VBAT (1 - VBAT/VCC) / (f L) (DS p8): 6 V / 3.7 V: 3.7 x 0.383 / (300 kHz x 47 uH) = 1.418 / 14.1 = 0.101 A (reproduces pinout.md:303). Maximum at VBAT = VCC/2 with VCC 8 V: 4 x 0.5 / 14.1 = 0.142 A; with f = 240 kHz (DS p4 min) and L = 42.3 uH: 2 / 10.15 = 0.197 A. Peak = 0.130/0.4653 (VCS max, RCS -1 %) + 0.197/2 = 0.279 + 0.099 = 0.378 A, 53 % of the 0.71 A IDC (CY54 DS p3); with a 5 % RCS 0.291 + 0.099 = 0.39 A. OK.
- AP2301 gate: CN3791 drive is typically 6.3 V peak-to-peak and clamped at 8 V below VCC (DS p7, p8), so |Vgs| <= 8 V against +-12 V (AP2301 DS p1). Vth -0.5 to -1.0 V, logic-level as DS p8 requires. Rds(on) 75 mohm max at -4.5 V (AP2301 DS p2). M1 loss about 21 mW (PA-4) x 125 C/W (DS p1) = 2.6 C rise. OK.

- COM network: 120 ohm + 235 nF (2 x 470 nF X7R +-10 % in series: 211-259 nF) vs 120 ohm + 220 nF (DS p2, p7). OK. DONE tied to GND matches DS p7 ("open drain status output that is not used should be tied to ground").
- C1 composition (electrolytic + 1-10 uF ceramic + 47 nF-1 uF, DS p8) matches 100 uF + 10 uF + 470 nF. Input ripple rating needed about ICH/2 = 0.128 A rms (DS p8); most of it flows in the 10 uF ceramic.

### B. TP4056 (YONGYUTAI)
- VCC minimum and UVLO: operating 4.35-6.5 V, UVLO 3.45-3.8 V rising, VCC-VBAT lockout 25-65 mV falling (DS p3). USB source >= 4.75 V (USB 2.0 / Type-C vSafe5V). - CE tied to VCC: CE abs max 8 V, VCE-H >= 1.5 V (DS p1, p3). OK.

### C. Two chargers on VBAT
- No current into a disconnected panel: D1 blocks VBAT from the M1 body diode and VCC (CN3791 DS p9); the MPPT divider (500 k) sits on the panel side of D1. Only D1/D2 leakage remains (accepted decision).
- No current into a disconnected USB input from VBAT: TP4056 anti-reverse, <= 3 uA (YONGYUTAI DS p1, p3). The only VBUS back-feed is from D+ (PC-2).
- USB removed while the sun is up: TP4056 enters shutdown when VCC falls within the VASD window of VBAT (DS p3-p4), CN3791 keeps charging. Sun lost while USB is present: CN3791 sleeps when VCC - VBAT < 0.0-0.1 V (CN3791 DS p4), D1 blocks; TP4056 keeps charging.
- CN3791 with VBAT held up by the TP4056 (and the reverse): each charger regulates its own float (TP4056 4.158-4.25 V, CN3791 4.158-4.247 V); the higher one carries the CV current and the other terminates at its own threshold (TP4056 C/10; CN3791 16 % of 0.255 A = 41 mA), then each restarts below its recharge level (TP4056 4.05-4.15 V, CN3791 0.955 x 4.2 = 4.011 V). No state drives VBAT above 4.25 V; the CN3791 also turns M1 off above 1.04-1.10 x VREG (CN3791 DS p4, p5).
- Night drain of the chargers: TP4056 <= 3 uA (DS p3); CN3791 BAT pin <= 15 uA max at 3.7 V (DS p4, IBAT2), about 9 uA BAT + CSP typical at 4.2 V (DS p9), plus D1/D2 leakage (accepted). Used in PH-1.

### D. Battery and PCM
- Over-charge: neither charger can push VBAT above 4.25 V (TP4056 VFLOAT max, YONGYUTAI DS p3; CN3791 VREG max 4.247 V and OVP, DS p4); a typical PCM backs this up at 4.28 +- 0.08 V (nuisance-trip note in PB-5).
- Short circuit on the PCB side: only the PCM protects (a typical spec requires PCM short-circuit protection, p11 of the representative spec); JST PH is rated 2 A (`hardware/pinout.md:413`); the 0.3 mm power-trace rule is about 1 A at dT = 10 C (`hardware/PCB_REGLAS.md:24`) against 0.885 A worst-case simultaneous charge. OK; a wider VBAT trunk from J2 is cheap margin.
- Over-discharge: EN tied to IN means the rail follows the cell down to the PCM cut (typical 2.8 +- 0.15 V); this is the deferred EN-supervisor decision and is not re-reported.
- Battery hot-plug with C3 + TP4056 BAT + TLV IN = 40 uF of ceramics: small inrush, no PCM concern; the only rule is "battery before panel" (CN3791 DS p7-8).

## Cross-domain notes

- **MCU:** (1) Deep-sleep pad states decide RNF-03: GPIO8/9 (I2C, 4.7k pull-ups) must not be driven low or pulled down (2 x 3.3 V / 4.7k = 1.4 mA); if UART RX or EXTINT is set as the UBX-RXM-PMREQ wake-up source, GPIO17 (GNSS RXD) and GPIO21 (EXTINT) must be held at a stable level in deep sleep, or a glitch wakes the MAX-M10S from software standby (46 uA vs 13.8 mA; MAX-M10S integration manual p47); GPIO38-40 (LED cathodes, not RTC pads, anodes at 3V3) must not float low or be pulled down. (2) GPIO41 CHRG pull-up is 10k: the CN3791 sinks 3.3 V / 10k = 330 uA from 3V3 for the whole charging day; 100k is enough for a status line (CHRG leakage <= 1 uA, CN3791 DS p4). (3) PD-1 and PD-2 fixes need parts (AP2301, an N-FET) and one GPIO (GPIO48 is free in phase 1). (4) USB D+ pull-up back-feed (PC-2, PC-U1).
- **FIRMWARE:** awake-time budget of at most about 1.5 h/day for RNF-01 and the 60-minute timer events in the budget (PH-2); charge-temperature inhibit above about 45 C via the IMU temperature sensor (PD-2); extend the low-battery rule to GNSS fixes (with a cold cell the 0.1 A fix phase also approaches the 3.0 V limit below about 3.1 V at rest); VRESET.Dis = 1 confirmed (MAX17048 DS p2).

- **BOM:** X7R 10 uF at TP4056 VCC (PB-3); 1 % 10k in R3 (PA-1); AP2301 x1-2 and a small N-FET for PD-1/PD-2; RCS must be the 1 % part of the O22 kit ("1 % o 5 %"); rail capacitance is 64.5 uF, not 55 uF (PE-1); the HRK spec in the repo is for a 33 uF / 400 V part, not the 100 uF / 35 V C1 (PA-U1); AP2301 values in COMPRAS D5 match its p2 table.
