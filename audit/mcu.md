## Verified OK

- **A. Pin files:** a scripted cross-check (`python3 -I`) of all 41 module pins in `pinout.md:63-105`, `esp32-s3-wroom-1u_pins.csv` and the ESP32 rows of `pin_connections.csv` against WROOM DS Table 3-1 (pp.11-12) found no pin-number, GPIO or net mismatch, no duplicated net and no missing pad. The GPIO map (`pinout.md:21-55`) gives the same GPIO-to-pad pairs. Peripheral-side rows agree with the GPIO side (LoRa pads 5-19, GNSS pins 2/3/5, IMU 4/13/14, MAX17048 7/8, CN3791 pin 3, VO617A pin 1, LED cathodes, USBLC6 pins 4/6).
- **A. Peripheral routing on IO MUX:** UART1 on GPIO17/18 (U1TXD/U1RXD) and SPI2 on GPIO10-13 (FSPICS0/FSPID/FSPICLK/FSPIQ) are the IO MUX default functions (WROOM DS Table 3-1 p11), so both buses avoid the GPIO matrix and its speed limit.
- **B. Boot mode:** GPIO0 has only a 10 kΩ pull-up, SW3 to GND and TP2, and no capacitor (HDG 1.3.9 p15 warns against high-value capacitors). GPIO46 is NC with an internal pull-down. Result: SPI Boot when SW3 is released and Joint Download Boot when GPIO0 = 0 at EN release (WROOM DS Table 4-3 p14). `pinout.md:111,444-451` describe this correctly.
- **B. VDD_SPI:** GPIO45 is NC with an internal pull-down, so VDD_SPI is 3.3 V from VDD3P3_RTC through RSPI with the default eFuse (WROOM DS Table 4-4 p15). This is correct for the N16R8 (3.3 V quad flash and octal PSRAM; the 1.8 V case only applies to R16V parts, WROOM DS p4 note 7 and p12 note c).
- **B. EN timing:** 10 kΩ and 1 µF give τ = 10 kΩ x 1 µF = 10 ms. EN crosses VIH_nRST = 0.75 x VDD at t = τ x ln(1/(1 - 0.75)) = 10 ms x 1.386 = 13.9 ms, against tSTBL ≥ 50 µs (WROOM DS Table 4-6 p16). The rail ramp of about 550 µs claimed in `pinout.md:110` is far shorter than 13.9 ms. Strapping hold tH ≥ 3 ms (Table 4-2 p14) is met because the strapping levels are static pulls. SW2 in parallel with the 1 µF holds EN low for as long as it is pressed, which is far longer than tRST ≥ 50 µs.
- **B. Glitches on safety-relevant outputs:** GPIO1 (OPTO_CTRL) and GPIO2 (BUZZER) glitch low (S3 DS Table 2-2), which is their off state, so no false actuation at power-up. GPIO21 (GNSS_EXTINT), GPIO38-42 and GPIO47 have no listed glitch.
- **B. Cold power-up contention:** at power-up the SX1262 is in Reset/Startup, with BUSY a 50 kΩ pull-up, DIO1 a 50 kΩ pull-down and NSS/MOSI/SCK inputs (SX1262 DS Table 8-3 p54). IMU INT1 defaults to "output forced to ground" (LSM6DS3TR-C DS Table 18 p47). The GNSS PIOs are inputs with pull-up in reset (MAX-M10S DS Table 11 p10). So the low glitches on GPIO4/6/14 meet only pull resistors or a low output: no push-pull contention.
- **C. USB polarity and parts:** connector D+ (A6/B6) → USBLC6 pin 1 → pin 6 → 22 Ω → module pin 14 = GPIO20 = USB_D+; D- (A7/B7) → pin 3 → pin 4 → 22 Ω → module pin 13 = GPIO19 = USB_D- (`hardware/pinout.md:386-398`; WROOM DS Table 3-1 p11; HDG 1.3.13 p18). The series value is within the HDG's 22/33 Ω. The USBLC6 sits at the connector and the resistors at the module (HDG p18, USBLC6 DS p5). CC1 and CC2 each have their own 5.1 kΩ Rd (sink).
- **C. VBUS:** goes only to USBLC6 pin 5 and TP4056 VCC, never to the LDO or the 3V3 rail (`hardware/pinout.md:112,384`; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:11`). With battery and USB both connected, the module runs from the LDO and enumerates normally.
- **D. Addresses:** IMU SA0 to GND gives 0x6A (LSM6DS3TR-C DS p39). MAX17048 is fixed at 0x36 (`pinout.md:259`). No conflict.
- **D. Logic levels (VDD = 3.3 V):** ESP32 input VIH = 0.75 x 3.3 = 2.475 V and VIL = 0.25 x 3.3 = 0.825 V (WROOM DS Table 6-3 p27). The lowest driven level on the bus is the MAX17048 VOL of 0.4 V at 4 mA (DS p2), below 0.825 V. The IMU's is 0.2 V at 4 mA (DS p24). The IMU input needs VIH ≥ 0.7 x 3.3 = 2.31 V and VIL ≤ 0.99 V (DS p24). The MAX17048 needs VIH ≥ 1.4 V and VIL ≤ 0.5 V (DS p2). The ESP32 drives VOL ≤ 0.1 x 3.3 = 0.33 V, below 0.5 V with 0.17 V margin. Sink current with 4.7 kΩ is 3.3 V / 4.7 kΩ = 0.70 mA, which keeps every VOL well under its rated 4 mA value. Line leakage (MAX17048 IPD 0.4 µA max per line, ESP32 IIH 50 nA) drops at most 0.45 µA x 4.7 kΩ = 2 mV on the high level.
- **D. MAX17048 on VBAT with a 3.3 V bus:** SDA/SCL are rated to 6 V absolute ("All other pins") and VIH is 1.4 V (MAX17048 DS p2). The bus works for any VBAT from 2.5 to 4.5 V, with no back-powering through the pins in either direction. Sleep from a held-low bus needs MODE.EnSleep = 1 (DS p10), which is 0 by default. The 60 µs power-up glitch on GPIO8/9 is far shorter than tSLEEP (1.75 s min).
- **D. IMU pins in I2C mode:** CS to VDDIO selects I2C. SDO/SA0 has no pull-up by default and is tied to GND. SDx/SCx go to GND, which the datasheet allows ("Connect to VDDIO or GND"). INT2 defaults to "output forced to ground", so it can be left open. Pins 10/11 have internal pull-ups but are unconnected, so they draw no current (LSM6DS3TR-C DS Table 18 pp.47-48). INT1 is push-pull, low by default.
- **D. Pull-up rail:** 3V3 is always on while VBAT is present (TLV75733P EN tied to IN, `pinout.md:237`), so the bus is never pulled to an unpowered rail while the MAX17048 runs. Sleep cost is about 2 x 0.4 µA = 0.8 µA (MAX17048 IPD).
- **E. SPI mode and levels:** the SX1262 uses CPOL = 0, CPHA = 0, and MISO is high-impedance while NSS is high (SX1262 DS p50). SPI2 IO MUX pins carry it with no matrix delay. SX1262 inputs need VIH ≥ 0.7 x 3.3 = 2.31 V (DS p21); the ESP32 gives VOH ≥ 0.8 x 3.3 = 2.64 V. NRESET needs VIL ≤ 0.2 x 3.3 = 0.66 V; the ESP32 gives VOL ≤ 0.33 V.
- **E. BUSY:** in Sleep/Startup BUSY is high-impedance with a pull-up, and an output in every other mode (SX1262 DS Table 8-3 p54). No pull-down is fitted on GPIO14, which is correct: a 45 kΩ internal pull-down against the 20 kΩ pull-up of p52 would draw 3.3 V / (20 kΩ + 45 kΩ) = 51 µA. The SX1262 datasheet itself gives 20 kΩ in the text (p52) but 50 kΩ in Table 8-3 (p54). `pinout.md:216` uses 20 kΩ, the conservative value: 3.3 V / 20 kΩ = 165 µA if hard-pulled low.
- **E. RESET:** SX1262 NRESET has an internal 50 kΩ pull-up (Table 8-3). `pinout.md:217` says not to drive it low in sleep: 3.3 V / 50 kΩ = 66 µA if it were.
- **E. DIO1:** in Sleep and Startup the SX1262 pulls DIO1 down with about 50 kΩ (Table 8-3), so the GPIO6 wake input has a defined low with no external part.
- **E. DIO2 / TXEN option:** the 0 Ω on pad 7 plus the DNP 0 Ω from DIO2 to pad 7 lets the board match either vendor variant (manual p11 Figure 2, p14). The rule to remove the pad-7 0 Ω when DIO2 is used (`pinout.md:224`) prevents push-pull contention between GPIO47 and DIO2.
- **F. UART crossing:** GPIO17 = U1TXD → MAX-M10S RXD (pin 3); GPIO18 = U1RXD ← TXD (pin 2) (`pinout.md:40-41,161-162`; WROOM DS Table 3-1 p11).
- **F. Levels:** with V_IO = 3.3 V the GNSS needs Vih ≥ 0.68 x 3.3 = 2.244 V and Vil ≤ 0.63 V (MAX-M10S DS p12); the ESP32 drives VOH ≥ 2.64 V and VOL ≤ 0.33 V. The GNSS drives Voh ≥ 3.3 - 0.4 = 2.9 V and Vol ≤ 0.4 V at 2 mA, against ESP32 VIH 2.475 V and VIL 0.825 V. V_IO is tied to VCC with VIO_SEL open (3.3 V range, 2.7 V to VCC, DS p12).
- **F. Power-up:** TIMEPULSE has no load, so nothing can pull it low at start-up and force safeboot (IM p29). SAFEBOOT_N goes only to a test pad. RESET_N is open, with no capacitor (IM p28). EXTINT (GPIO21) has no ESP32 power-up glitch (S3 DS Table 2-2) and no pull at reset, so the GNSS pull-up holds it high. V_IO ramp: about 550 µs / 3.3 V = 167 µs/V, inside 25-35 000 µs/V (DS p11; the 550 µs comes from `pinout.md:176`, not re-measured).
- **F. Standby:** in software standby the GNSS TXD becomes an input with pull-up (DS Table 11 p10), so GPIO18 stays high with no contention. The "PIOs must not be driven in hardware backup" rule does not apply, because VCC and V_IO are never removed.
- **G. Optocoupler input (GPIO1):** IF = (3.3 - 1.1) / 330 Ω = 6.7 mA typical. Worst case with VOH = 0.8 x 3.3 = 2.64 V and VF = 1.2 V: (2.64 - 1.2) / 330 Ω = 4.4 mA. Both are below the 60 mA absolute maximum (VO617A DS p2) and within the ESP32's 40 mA IOH. The 100 kΩ pull-down holds the LED off whenever the pad floats (reset, deep sleep without hold) and costs 3.3 V / 100 kΩ = 33 µA only while ON. Power-up glitch is low. Deep sleep: RTC hold low (`pinout.md:440`, `BOOT_SEQUENCE.md:89`), 0 µA.
- **G. RGB LED (active low, anodes on 3V3):** I = (3.3 - VF - 0.04) / R. Red (3.3 - 1.83 - 0.04) / 750 = 1.91 mA; green (3.3 - 2.35 - 0.04) / 470 = 1.94 mA; blue (3.3 - 2.62 - 0.04) / 330 = 1.94 mA. Total 5.8 mA, far below the 28 mA IOL rating (WROOM DS Table 6-3 p27) and the 20 mA default drive (S3 DS note 5 p18). At reset GPIO38/40 are inputs and GPIO39 (MTCK) has its weak pull-up (S3 DS Table 2-1 note 7), so all three LEDs are off with no current through the anode-side path.
- **G. Buzzer (GPIO2, 100 Ω, piezo about 9 nF):** ESP32 source resistance ≤ (3.3 - 2.64) V / 40 mA = 16.5 Ω, so the peak edge current is 3.3 V / (100 + 16.5) Ω = 28 mA (33 mA with an ideal driver), decaying with τ = 116.5 Ω x 9 nF = 1.05 µs. Average current = C x V x f = 9 nF x 3.3 V x 4 kHz = 0.12 mA, matching `pinout.md:337`. No DC path in sleep.
- **G. User button (GPIO7):** on release C charges through 10 kΩ + 1 kΩ, so τ = 11 kΩ x 100 nF = 1.1 ms and VIH (0.75 x VDD) is reached at 1.1 ms x ln 4 = 1.52 ms. On press C discharges through 1 kΩ, so τ = 0.1 ms and VIL (0.25 x VDD) is reached at 0.1 ms x ln 4 = 0.14 ms. Pressed current 3.3 V / 10 kΩ = 0.33 mA. Released there is no DC path: only pad leakage IIH ≤ 50 nA (WROOM DS Table 6-3) plus MLCC leakage. Firmware debounce of 20 ms is planned (`pinout.md:366`).
- **H. Wake pins:** GPIO4, GPIO6 and GPIO7 are RTC_GPIO4/6/7 (WROOM DS Table 3-1 p11), inside the GPIO0-21 RTC range. The pins that need a hold in sleep (GPIO1 OPTO low, GPIO10 NSS high) are also RTC pads. Keeping ext0/ext1 and RTC holds powered means "RTC memory and RTC peripherals powered up", 8 µA typical (WROOM DS Table 6-7 p30), which is the figure the budget uses (`docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md:130`).
- **H. Wake-pin idle levels without external parts:** IMU INT1 is push-pull, low when idle (LSM6DS3TR-C DS p47). DIO1 is pulled down by the SX1262 in Sleep (Table 8-3). BUTTON idles high through 11 kΩ. None needs an internal pull.
- **H. Hardware sleep-current paths (as designed, firmware rules met):** OPTO 0 µA (held low); buzzer 0 (capacitive); IMU INT1, DIO1, BUSY, NRESET, EXTINT and GNSS RXD 0 (inputs facing pull resistors only); I2C about 0.8 µA (MAX17048 IPD); button 0 when released; LEDs 0 (pads high-Z, anode-side LED reverse path nil); EN, BOOT 0 (no DC path); CHRG_STAT 0 at night (CN3791 not charging), 330 µA by day (G-1). Open items: RXEN/TXEN (E-2) and the USB D+ path (C-3).
- **I. First flash with an empty flash:** a new module ships with its memory erased (WROOM DS 6.5 p30), so the first download needs Joint Download Boot (HDG 1.5 p35 step 1). Hold SW3, press and release SW2, release SW3, with VBAT supplied. After that, USB Serial/JTAG on GPIO19/20 or UART0 on TP3/TP4 both work; the log shows "waiting for download". `pinout.md:449-451` documents this correctly.
- **I. Reflashing over USB without buttons:** this works while the running app keeps the USB Serial/JTAG peripheral and GPIO19/20 untouched (HDG 1.5 p36). The planned phase-2 "USB CDC" console only keeps this if it uses the USB Serial/JTAG CDC, not a USB-OTG device stack (`pinout.md:451` already warns).
- **I. UART0:** TXD0 has the 499 Ω (plan B 480 Ω) series resistor of HDG 1.3.7 p13. RC with a 50 pF adapter input = 499 Ω x 50 pF = 25 ns, far below one bit at 921 600 baud (1.09 µs). UART0 and USB both carry ROM messages by default (WROOM DS 4.3 p15).
- **I. Power-up cases:** battery only is the normal case. Battery and USB together: the module runs from the LDO, the TP4056 charges, and USB enumerates. USB only: undefined, see C-1.
- **I. Access, lid open vs sealed:** sealed, only the USB-C (charging and USB Serial/JTAG, with the battery inside) and the user button through its membrane. Lid open: SW2/TP1 (EN), SW3/TP2 (BOOT), TP3/TP4 (UART0), 3V3/GND pads, the GNSS SAFEBOOT_N pad, the battery connector (series current measurement) and the antenna U.FLs.

## Cross-domain notes

- **POWER:** (1) USB-only operation is undefined: the TP4056 "no battery" mode cycles VBAT between 4.05 and 4.2 V, and a 70 mA step can pull the node below the LDO's needs in less than tRECHARGE (C-1). (2) With battery and USB both present, the TP4056 counts the system load as charge current. An awake ESP32 at 40-80 mA is near or above C/10 = 50 mA, so charging may never terminate and the cell sits at the 4.2 V float through long USB sessions. (3) The D+ pull-up back-feeds the VBUS node through the USBLC6 into TP4056 VCC (shutdown ICC 55/100 µA) (C-3). (4) CHRG_STAT pull-up costs 330 µA while the CN3791 charges (G-1). (5) GNSS VCC is always on, so its sleep current depends on firmware PMREQ standby; there is no way to power-cycle it (F-1).
- **RF:** the module uses a crystal, not a TCXO, so DIO3 stays NC and `SetDIO3AsTCXOCtrl` must not be called (E-3). The RXEN/TXEN truth table and deep-sleep level are unknown (E-2, E-4). Both antennas must be fitted before any RF bring-up (I-5). SCK RC footprint for EMC (E-5).
- **BOM:** if accepted, add: 100 kΩ NSS pull-up (E-1), 2 x 100 kΩ RXEN/TXEN pull-downs (E-2), 0 Ω + DNP capacitor on SCK (E-5), 2 DNP capacitors on D+/D- (C-2), 0 Ω links for current measurement (I-3), optional 0 Ω GPIO48 to GNSS RESET_N (F-1), CHRG_STAT pull-up changed to 100 kΩ (G-1; `BOM.md:106` would then list 3 x 10 kΩ), optional 2.2 kΩ I2C pull-ups (D-1), extra test pads (F-2, I-4, I-6).
- **FIRMWARE:** I2C at 100 kHz (`BOOT_SEQUENCE.md:23` says 400 kHz) and bus recovery (D-1, D-2). Full peripheral re-init after any reset, with NRESET ≥ 1 ms and a BUSY wait (B-2). A deep-sleep pin table with pulls and holds (H-1, H-2, F-3). Pull-down on GPIO3 (B-1). Never drive RXEN and TXEN high together (E-4). Service mode on a long button press for sealed reflashing (I-1). Radios off until antennas are confirmed (I-5). Do not burn JTAG or USB eFuses (B-1, G-2).
- **MECH-REPORT:** SW2 and SW3 sit inside the sealed enclosure, so field reprogramming depends on the I-1 service mode, and the IP67 spec's "programación por USB nativo" (`mechanical/IP67_SPEC.md:28`) is only true with that firmware support. Keep SW2 and SW3 where the lid, gasket or potting cannot press them: a held BOOT forces download mode on every reset, and a held EN keeps the chip off. The `.tex` report should carry the same corrections as `pinout.md` for E-3 (crystal, RXEN/TXEN) and C-1 (USB-only behaviour).
